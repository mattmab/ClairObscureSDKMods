Enemy HP 0.1.4

Enable Enemy HP in Mods. Current HP appears on the game's normal enemy and
boss combat health bars, using the original widget's visibility and opacity.
HP sits above the upper-left end of the bar, offset left to clear burning/status icons and its break meter.
Numbers are grouped for readability and refresh up to twenty times per second.
Reusable UI schemas prepare at activation; labels can attach during battle intros.
No extra hotkey, combat changes, or save/map writes.

Requires ClairObscurSDK RC23. Disable the mod to remove its labels.

Uses SDK fast-refresh scheduling when available; older RC23 hosts use ordinary polling.
